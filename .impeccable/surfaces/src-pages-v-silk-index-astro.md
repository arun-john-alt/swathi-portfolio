# Surface brief: Silk version of Swathi's portfolio (/v/silk/)

Scope: a full replacement-world version of the portfolio home page, built beside the current site so the owner can choose between finished versions. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: Tamil Nadu business owners opening Swathi's link from an Instagram DM or WhatsApp on an Android phone, deciding whether to hire her. Owner's answers (11 Nov 2026): they should come away believing **her sites look premium**; every version must keep **all five live projects** (Kattam, Tunic Closet, Vastara the Label, 10X Healthy Skin, Rave Adventures). Reel, logo and copy may change; no invented facts, numbers, clients or quotes.

Action: message Swathi on Instagram (@swathi_krithvi), the only contact channel today.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a Kanjivaram silk laid out on a silk-house counter: her live sites are unfolded one by one, the way a salesman unfolds sarees for a customer, because to a Tamil Nadu shop owner Kanjivaram is what "premium" looks like. It refuses the designer-portfolio template (big name, dark mode, grid of case cards) and the cream, serif and terracotta default.

OWN-WORLD: Saree anatomy is the system. The body (udal) is a drenched peacock blue-green silk field with a slow, bounded sheen. The korvai contrast border in rani pink interlocks with the body through a row of temple-tooth (gopuram) triangles. Zari is antique gold thread, drawn as fine parallel lines, never as gradients, glow or gradient text. The pallu, the dense motif-woven end piece, carries the contact action. The work is laid on white cotton counter cloth with a faint plain-weave texture. Type: Rozha One (Indian Type Foundry's Didone) for display; Catamaran (Tamil and Latin, by Pria Ravichandran) for text; one Tamil greeting is allowed. Controls are woven tabs edged in zari; active means gold.

STORY: The owner sees a familiar, premium object, reads one line about what Swathi does, sees five live sites unfolded on the counter (desktop and phone), and taps the pallu's "Message me on Instagram".

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone (390): a zari-edged header strip with the logo; the peacock body fills the screen; a rani-pink korvai border runs down the left edge (about 18% of the width) with temple teeth biting into the body; the headline in Rozha One at about 44px, three or four lines; directly under it the primary action, a pink button edged in gold zari, "Message me on Instagram"; at the bottom edge the first folded project peeks up from the counter. Desktop (1440): the border is a wide band down the left; the headline sits left of centre; on the right the first project lies half unfolded, desktop and phone screenshots visible.

FORM: Kanjivaram zari border, position 4 of my 7 grounded candidates (assigned by the roll). seed:a6d24fcab05c655e. Signature interaction, "the weave": on load the zari threads draw across the border in sequence and the temple teeth interlock; on scroll each project unfolds (folded to flat) and the counter edge takes that project's colour. Raises: from the chromatophore skin, state lives in the surface colour (each unfolded project's border takes its client's own brand colour); from the blackbook page, motion is a thread drawn in sequence, never a fade-up; from the silkscreen loft, every project is shown the same way so only the work varies; from the arcade screen, one module (the temple tooth's width is the spacing unit); from the nixie counter, the few numbers sit in fixed places.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

Phones first: 360 px works, 44 px tap targets, nothing scrolls sideways, works without JavaScript and with reduced motion, readable on low-end Android. Lives under the GitHub Pages base path /swathi-portfolio/. DESIGN.md is written for whichever version the owner picks.
