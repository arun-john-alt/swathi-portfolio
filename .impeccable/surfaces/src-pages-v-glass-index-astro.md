# Surface brief: Glass version of Swathi's portfolio (/v/glass/)

Scope: a full replacement-world version of the portfolio home page, built beside the current site so the owner can choose between finished versions. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: Tamil Nadu business owners opening Swathi's link from an Instagram DM or WhatsApp on an Android phone, deciding whether to hire her. Owner's answers (11 Nov 2026): they should come away believing **her sites look premium**; every version must keep **all five live projects** (Kattam, Tunic Closet, Vastara the Label, 10X Healthy Skin, Rave Adventures). Reel, logo and copy may change; no invented facts, numbers, clients or quotes.

Action: message Swathi on Instagram (@swathi_krithvi), the only contact channel today.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is one steel-framed glass partition wall, the kind that fronts a modern showroom or clinic: each live site is a pane you step up to and see through, and the one saturated cobalt pane is the way to reach her. Premium here is clarity: structure holds, colour speaks. It refuses the template portfolio grid of case cards, the near-black-and-neon look and the cream-and-serif default.

OWN-WORLD: Matte black glazing bars are the only line ink, at true thickness (about 4 px on phones, 6 px on desktop, with heavier mullions between sections). Clear and seeded-white glass panes are the ground, textured with a fine seeded grain and a hand-rolled ripple. Cobalt, amber and oxblood glass are reserved for what matters now: cobalt is the action, amber the project in focus, oxblood the close. Type: Sofia Sans Extra Condensed for display, set inside panes and never across a bar; Sofia Sans for text. Hierarchy comes from pane size and glass colour more than type weight. Controls are panes: hover backlights a pane, selection floods it with its colour, disabled frosts to translucent white.

STORY: The owner sees a clean, premium wall, reads the offer in the largest pane, looks through five panes at five live sites (desktop and phone), and taps the cobalt pane to message her.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone (390): the wall elevation stacked; a top row of panes holds the logo pane (its blue square becomes a real cobalt pane) and a menu pane; a tall clear pane carries the headline in Sofia Sans Extra Condensed at about 64px; a narrow cobalt column beside it; under it a wide cobalt pane, "Message me on Instagram"; below, the first project pane begins, its live screenshot seen through seeded glass. Desktop (1440): the full elevation, with a left column of navigation panes, the headline pane in the centre, project panes on the right.

FORM: Glazier colourfield partition, catalog challenger craft-making-glazier-colorfield-partition, verdict competitive (holds product clarity); built as the second version because the owner chooses between finished versions. seed:a6d24fcab05c655e. Signature interaction, "daylight to backlight": on load the wall is almost clear, panes brighten in sequence, then the cobalt pane lights from behind and holds; on scroll the pane nearest the centre brightens and its site comes through sharp while the others stay seeded. Nothing slides.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

Phones first: 360 px works, 44 px tap targets, nothing scrolls sideways, works without JavaScript and with reduced motion, readable on low-end Android. Lives under the GitHub Pages base path /swathi-portfolio/. DESIGN.md is written for whichever version the owner picks.
